About Me

My name is Emmanuel Benard and I am a first-year Computer Science student at the University of Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. I was born and raised in Tanzania and I have always had a strong passion for technology and how it can be used to solve everyday problems in our society.

I am currently pursuing my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science where I study subjects such as Discrete Mathematics, Computer Organization and Architecture, C Programming, and Web Development. These courses have helped me build a strong foundation in both the theoretical and practical sides of computing.

My dream is to become a skilled software developer and contribute to the growth of technology in East Africa. I believe that with determination and hard work, technology can transform lives and communities across the continent.
